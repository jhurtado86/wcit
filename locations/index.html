Planning your visit

Before you come in

Request an appointment and our team will call you to set up your first visit at the clinic that is closer to you. Both clinics keep the same hours, Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, and share one phone number.

If your doctor, hospital, or nursing facility is referring you, they send the referral to either clinic through our secure LeadingReach portal or by fax to (956) 800-4275, and our team calls you from there.

Wondering what a first visit is like, or what to bring? Our patient resources page walks you through it, from check-in to caring for your wound at home.
