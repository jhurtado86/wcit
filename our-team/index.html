Certified wound specialists

A multidisciplinary team with certified wound specialists

Two of our clinicians are certified by the American Board of Wound Management: Dr. José R. Sobrevilla, MD, CWSP, a Certified Wound Specialist Physician, and Cesar Martinez, RN, CWS, a Certified Wound Specialist. They work alongside our physicians and nurse practitioners, who bring wound care and critical care experience.

Dr. Pedro Abanto, MD, FACP, is an internal medicine physician who holds the Essentials of Wound Care and Hyperbaric Medicine certification. Our three nurse practitioners came to wound care from intensive care, long-term acute care, and clinical leadership.

Every plan is built by the team and reviewed at every visit. All of our care is outpatient and evidence-based, with a plan built around your wound and your overall health.

Physicians

Our physicians

Dr. José R. Sobrevilla, MD, CWSP

Physician · Certified Wound Specialist Physician · Hospitalist and Family Medicine

Dr. Sobrevilla is a family medicine physician and a Certified Wound Specialist Physician, a diplomate of the American Board of Wound Management. He trained at the Universidad de Montemorelos in Nuevo León, Mexico, completed his internship at ISSSTE Monterrey, and came to the Valley for the McAllen Family Medicine Residency Program. His career has moved from hospitalist and clinic medicine into wound healing centers and, now, wound care at our clinics, where he examines each wound, looks for the reason it has not healed, and builds the plan to treat it.

Dr. Pedro Abanto, MD, FACP

Physician · Internal Medicine · Wound Care and Hyperbaric Medicine

Dr. Abanto is an internal medicine physician, board certified by the American Board of Internal Medicine and a Fellow of the American College of Physicians. He holds the Essentials of Wound Care and Hyperbaric Medicine certification. He trained at the Universidad de Montemorelos and completed his internal medicine residency at the Regional Academic Health Center of the UT Health Science Center at San Antonio, in Harlingen, Texas. He brings an internist’s view of the whole patient to wound care: the diabetes, circulation, and heart conditions that so often keep a wound from closing.

Wound care nursing

Our certified wound specialist nurse

Cesar Martinez, RN, CWS

Registered Nurse · Certified Wound Specialist, American Board of Wound Management

Cesar Trinidad Martinez Cavada is a registered nurse and a Certified Wound Specialist of the American Board of Wound Management. He earned his BSN and MSN at the Escuela de Enfermería Dr. José Ángel Cadena y Cadena in Reynosa, Mexico, and also holds a Médico Cirujano Partero degree from UMAN. His role centers on wound care for patients in nursing facilities: wound assessments, assisting with debridements, and teaching patients and families how to care for a wound between visits.

Nurse practitioners

Our nurse practitioners

Three family nurse practitioners who came to wound care from intensive care, long-term acute care, and clinical leadership.

  • Andrea Garza, FNP-C

    Family Nurse Practitioner · MSN

    Andrea Garza is a certified family nurse practitioner with an intensive care background. Before becoming a nurse practitioner, she worked as a registered nurse in critical care and complex wound care at Rio Grande Regional, DHR, and Edinburg Regional. She earned her MSN as a family nurse practitioner from Chamberlain College of Nursing, with honors, and her BSN, cum laude, from the University of Texas–Pan American.

  • Patrizia Cantu, MSN, FNP-BC

    Family Nurse Practitioner (Board Certified) · MSN

    Patrizia Cantu is a board-certified family nurse practitioner. Much of her nursing career was spent as a dedicated wound care nurse in long-term acute care, treating diabetic, venous, arterial, and pressure ulcers with debridement, negative pressure therapy, and collagen dressings, with further time in the cardiovascular ICU and neuro-trauma units. She earned her MSN as a family nurse practitioner from Houston Baptist University and her BSN, with a biology and chemistry minor, from the University of Texas–Pan American.

  • Edwardo Bernal, FNP-BC

    Family Nurse Practitioner (Board Certified) · MSN

    Edwardo Bernal is a board-certified family nurse practitioner with a background in clinical leadership, including roles as a clinical director and administrator. His practice covers care across the lifespan, with a focus on the chronic conditions, such as diabetes and high blood pressure, that so often stand between a patient and a healed wound. He earned his MSN as a family nurse practitioner from Walden University and his BSN from the University of Texas–Pan American.
