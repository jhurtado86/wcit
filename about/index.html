Our mission

To enhance the lives and health of our patients through improved access to quality, affordable, and innovative wound care services.

How we work

We treat the wound, and the reason it will not heal

A wound that has not healed in weeks has a reason: pressure, poor blood flow, swelling, infection, nerve damage, or a condition such as diabetes. Our care starts there. A physician examines and measures the wound, looks for that reason, and builds a plan around it and around your overall health.

The plan draws on seven treatments, from debridement and offloading to compression, specialty dressings, negative pressure, skin substitutes, and hyperbaric oxygen therapy. The team reviews your wound at every visit and adjusts the plan as it changes.

All of it is outpatient. You come in for care and go home the same day, at either clinic, Monday through Friday. The goal is safe, effective, and comfortable care that gives you a greater chance of healing and a better quality of life.

Our team

Certified wound specialists on a multidisciplinary team

Two of our clinicians are certified by the American Board of Wound Management: Dr. José R. Sobrevilla, MD, CWSP, a Certified Wound Specialist Physician, and Cesar Martinez, RN, CWS, a Certified Wound Specialist. They work alongside our physicians and nurse practitioners, who bring wound care and critical care experience.

Dr. Pedro Abanto, MD, FACP, is an internal medicine physician with the Essentials of Wound Care and Hyperbaric Medicine certification. Our three family nurse practitioners came to wound care from intensive care, long-term acute care, and clinical leadership. Together they review every patient’s wound, visit after visit.

Referral network

A referral partner for the Valley’s hospitals and nursing facilities

We care for patients from more than 20 nursing facilities and 8 hospitals, and we partner with the South Texas Health System Wound Healing Center.

Most of our patients reach us by referral. Hospitals, nursing facilities, and physician offices across the Rio Grande Valley send their patients with chronic wounds to our clinics through LeadingReach, a secure referral platform, or by fax. Patients and families can also contact us directly.
