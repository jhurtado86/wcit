Why refer to us

A certified wound-specialty team, in an outpatient setting

Your patient is seen by a multidisciplinary team focused on chronic and non-healing wounds.

Two of our clinicians are certified by the American Board of Wound Management: Dr. José R. Sobrevilla, MD, CWSP, and Cesar Martinez, RN, CWS. They work alongside our physicians and nurse practitioners, who bring wound care and critical care experience.

Our seven services include hyperbaric oxygen therapy, with physician-supervised sessions. Dr. Pedro Abanto, MD, FACP, holds the Essentials of Wound Care and Hyperbaric Medicine certification.

All care is outpatient and evidence-based, at two clinics in Hidalgo County: McAllen and Mission. Patients are treated and go home the same day, and both clinics share one phone number and the same hours.

How to refer

How to refer, and what happens next

LeadingReach is our preferred channel. Fax works too.

  1. 1

    Send the referral

    Submit it through our secure LeadingReach portal, or fax it to (956) 800-4275. Questions before you send? Call (956) 317-4044 or (956) 317-4043.

  2. 2

    Your patient is seen in McAllen or Mission

    Outpatient visits run Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, at either clinic. Our multidisciplinary team examines and measures the wound and looks for the reason it has not healed.

  3. 3

    Treatment begins, as an outpatient

    The plan draws on debridement, offloading and compression, specialty dressings, and the advanced therapies: negative pressure, skin substitutes, and hyperbaric oxygen. Patients go home the same day after every visit.

Wound details, records, and orders travel through LeadingReach or by fax. Our website’s contact form is for patients and collects contact information only.

What we handle

Wound types and treatments

Refer any wound that has not started to heal in about 2 weeks or is not closed in about 6, and any open wound in a patient with diabetes, poor circulation, or vascular disease.

Wound types

  • Diabetic and neuropathic foot ulcers
  • Arterial (ischemic) ulcers
  • Venous stasis ulcers
  • Pressure ulcers
  • Non-healing surgical wounds, including post-amputation
  • Radiation wounds
  • Burns, after emergency care
  • Peristomal skin irritation
  • Fistulas
  • Other chronic and resistant wounds

Treatments

  • Hyperbaric oxygen therapy
  • Negative pressure wound therapy (wound VAC)
  • Debridement
  • Total contact casting
  • Bio-engineered skin substitutes
  • Compression therapy
  • Specialty dressings and ointments

Every plan starts with a physician examination. For coverage or authorization questions, call our office at (956) 317-4044.

Questions

Provider FAQ

Short answers for referral coordinators, case managers, and facility staff.

Which patients should I refer?

Any patient whose wound has not started to heal in about 2 weeks or has not closed in about 6, and any patient with diabetes, poor circulation, or vascular disease who has an open wound. If you are unsure, call us and describe the wound.

How do I send clinical information?

Through LeadingReach, or by fax to (956) 800-4275. Our website’s contact form is for patients and collects contact information only, so please do not send wound details or records through it.

Do you see patients who live in nursing facilities?

Yes. We care for patients from more than 20 nursing facilities across the Rio Grande Valley. Facility staff can refer through LeadingReach or by fax.

Do you offer hyperbaric oxygen therapy?

Yes. Hyperbaric oxygen therapy is one of our seven services, with physician-supervised sessions. It is used for select wounds, such as diabetic foot ulcers that have not improved with standard care and radiation injury. The hyperbaric oxygen therapy page lists the indications.

Can a patient contact you directly?

Yes. Patients and families can request an appointment through our contact page or call (956) 317-4044. Clinical referrals from providers still come through LeadingReach or fax.
